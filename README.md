# 🍽️ Zomato Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Zomato Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project created using **Microsoft Power BI**. It explores restaurant distribution across cities, customer ratings, pricing, online delivery availability, cuisines, and voting patterns.

The dashboard presents key metrics and charts in one place, making restaurant data easier to understand and helping identify useful business trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze restaurant distribution across cities.
- Understand restaurant ratings and customer preferences.
- Examine the average cost for two people.
- Compare restaurants across price ranges and rating categories.
- Explore online delivery availability.
- Analyze customer votes across rating levels.
- Present insights through clear dashboard visualizations.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

1. **Count of Restaurants by City** — compares restaurant counts across cities such as New Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, and Ghaziabad.
2. **Restaurants by Online Delivery** — shows the split between restaurants with and without online delivery.
3. **Average Rating by City** — compares the displayed city-level values.
4. **Rating by Price Range** — shows how restaurants are distributed across price categories.
5. **Restaurants by Rating Text** — groups restaurants into categories such as Average, Good, Very Good, Poor, and Not Rated.
6. **Total Votes by Rating** — shows how customer votes vary across rating levels.
